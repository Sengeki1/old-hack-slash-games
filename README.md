# Older third-person hack-and-slash games

115 individual games, expanded from the earlier series list. Years indicate the first release, which may differ by country. Platforms list the original main versions, with selected later ports noted.

**Player scores:** SC = SensCritique community average, across the platforms grouped on its game page, out of 10. JVC = Jeuxvideo.com readers, out of 20, with the rated platform specified. GF = GameFabrique user votes, out of 10; the small vote count is included. MC = Metacritic users, out of 10. These are different communities and should not be treated as one ranking. Scores were retrieved on September 16, 2026, including indexed snapshots that may be older; follow each score link for its source.

**Images:** Every game has cover art. The second-image column contains confirmed gameplay images where available and explicitly labelled gallery previews elsewhere. Gallery previews can include promotional art, alternate packaging, or cutscenes and are **not verified gameplay**. When a second image could not be retrieved, the cell links to its source gallery instead. Thus this is not a fully verified two-image-per-game catalogue. External images require internet access and may be blocked by their hosts. Cover art can represent a regional release or later port.

The earlier list's *Heavenly Guardian* (top-down action) and *Spartacus Legends* (arena fighting) are excluded. Adjacent action adventures, RPGs, and brawlers remain included and are described accordingly.
